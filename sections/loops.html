The Centerpiece

Three Control Loops

GPU workload management is not one scheduling problem. It is three problems operating at three different timescales. Conflating them creates oscillation — the autoscaler and the quota manager fight each other.

Three
Timescales

Outer Loop: Kueue — Entitlement

Minutes–hours. Which team is entitled to GPU capacity? Manages quotas, fair-share weights, borrowing, preemption.

Middle Loop: WVA/KEDA — Scaling

Seconds–minutes. How many replicas does this model need? Scales based on queue depth, KV-cache pressure, token throughput, latency.

Inner Loop: llm-d EPP — Routing

Milliseconds. Which replica handles this request? Routes to the replica with the most relevant KV-cache via KV-Cache Indexer.

Insight: Kueue sets the ceiling. WVA scales within it. llm-d routes within the live replicas.
