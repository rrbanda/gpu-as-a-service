Fleet Orchestration with ACM

Red Hat Advanced Cluster Management (ACM) extends GPUaaS across multiple OpenShift clusters, providing capabilities critical for enterprise GPU operations:

Fleet-wide GPU Visibility

Unified dashboard showing GPU availability, utilization, and queue depth across all clusters. Operators see a single pane of glass for all GPU resources — dev, staging, production, and burst clusters.

Policy-Based Workload Placement

Placement rules route workloads to clusters with matching GPU topology, data locality, and compliance zone. Training goes to clusters with NVLink; inference goes to clusters closest to users.

GitOps Configuration

Consistent Kueue policies, ResourceFlavors, and DRA DeviceClasses across all clusters via ArgoCD or OpenShift GitOps. Changes are auditable, version-controlled, and rollback-ready.

Environment Promotion

Models validated in dev clusters are promoted to production clusters through policy-gated pipelines. Same model, same config, different GPU topology — no manual reconfiguration.
