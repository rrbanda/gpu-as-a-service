Kubeflow Trainer v2 (TrainJob API)

GA with native Kueue integration. The TrainJob API simplifies distributed training by abstracting away PyTorch, TensorFlow, and other framework boilerplate.

TrainJob — distributed fine-tuning with Kueue admission 
apiVersion: trainer.kubeflow.org/v1
kind: TrainJob
metadata:
  name: llama-finetune
  labels:
    kueue.x-k8s.io/queue-name: team-a-queue
    kueue.x-k8s.io/priority-class: production
spec:
  runtimeRef:
    name: torch-distributed
  trainer:
    image: quay.io/rhoai/training:latest
    numProcPerNode: "4"
    resourcesPerNode:
      requests:
        nvidia.com/gpu: "4"
        memory: 128Gi

JIT Checkpointing — Survive Preemption

When Kueue preempts a training job, the pod receives SIGTERM. JIT checkpointing intercepts this signal, pauses training after the current optimizer step, and asynchronously saves model state to PVC or S3. On restart, training automatically resumes from the latest valid checkpoint.

Step 1

SIGTERM received → handler pauses after current optimizer step

Step 2

Async save via CUDA streams to PVC or S3 with sentinel file

Step 3

On restart, resume from latest valid checkpoint automatically

CodeFlare SDK + KubeRay

Python-native distributed workload submission. Data scientists submit training jobs, hyperparameter sweeps, and Ray Serve deployments from Jupyter notebooks without writing Kubernetes YAML. Integrates with Kueue for fair-share admission.

GPUDirect RDMA — Topology-Aware Training

Demonstrated result: 3x training speedup (5 hours → 1 hour 40 minutes) for distributed fine-tuning on OpenShift AI by enabling GPU-to-GPU communication over RoCE/InfiniBand, bypassing CPU entirely. Topology-aware scheduling ensures multi-GPU jobs land on nodes connected via NVLink or high-bandwidth fabric.

Why training matters for GPUaaS: Without fair-share scheduling (Kueue) and preemption-safe checkpointing (JIT), training jobs are either starved for GPUs or they monopolize the cluster. KFTv2 + Kueue + JIT checkpointing makes training a first-class, preemptible citizen of the GPU fleet.
